Posting Response

When a posting is submitted, LeadVentures will respond with JSON. The JSON indicates whether the posting was successfully submitted. A response does not indicate the final disposition of the posted lead (i.e., whether or not you will get paid for it). That is determined by the final recipient of the lead.

Here's an example of a lead that was submitted successfully:
{"status":"success", "testing":true}

When using the pre-ping you have 2 URL options: Full or Duplicate and Cap only.
Only one option should be submitted.

Full Pre-Ping URL: http://back2learn-api.leadhoop.com/v1/pings

Using the Full Pre-Ping will have the most accurate representation if the lead will be Accepted based on Validation, cap, and duplication checks.
We suggest that you post to as many fields as possible when using the Full option, required and optional where possible.

Duplicate and Cap Pre-Ping URL: http://back2learn-api.leadhoop.com/v2/dup_pings

Using the Duplicate and Cap Pre-Ping is a simple check with a faster response time, based only on cap and duplication checks. A lead may still be Invalid after submission based on Validations in place.
